How the radar scores — the model, in full

impliedValue() in lib/model.ts scores every protocol the same way, from the snapshot alone — no name is ever special-cased:

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth × category

  • growth = 1 + clamp(7-day change, −50%, +100%) ÷ 100 × 0.9
  • maturity = 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age in days, 1460) ÷ 1460 — longer with no token, more pent-up value (unknown age counts as 365 days)
  • breadth = 1 + 0.04 × min(chains − 1, 8) — multi-chain reach
  • category = 1.12 risk curators & capital allocators, 1.08 lending & CDP, 1.06 DEX & perps, 1.05 yield & RWA, 0.95 stablecoins, 1.00 otherwise

Coverage: the board holds all watchlist protocols plus every other rankable pre-token protocol below the $50M floor. Wrapped, staked, bridged and exchange products are excluded — they are a token that already trades in another form. Farm plans come from farmSteps(): the protocol’s own category, chains, TVL tier and age set the action, the capital, the hold and the cost.
